## Before you start

- What you need open, and which role you need.
- Anything that must already be true.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="First thing">
    Say where to click and what to type. One action per step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Second thing">
    If a choice appears, say which to pick and why in one line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- What you should see, and where (a screen, an inbox, a record in Zoho).
- How long it should take.

## If it did not work

- The one or two usual causes, each with a link to the page that fixes it.
