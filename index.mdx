This is the handbook for the systems that run Comfort Hub: Zoho, Supabase, the 7AM app, QuickBooks, the pay page, Stripe, Twilio and the email service. It says what each process does, how to do the everyday tasks, and what to do when something breaks.

It is written for the owner and the developers, not for customers. It uses plain words and the names people say out loud, never the names the databases use inside.

## How the handbook is arranged

The **Handbook** tab follows the order the business runs: a new customer, then booking, then the visit, then the invoice, then the payment, and on to parts, hours, the phone, and keeping it all running. Each chapter opens with one page that explains how that part works, then has short pages for each task.

The **Systems** tab is the same story told by system instead of by process. Go there when you are inside one tool and want to know what it owns and what it talks to.

The **Reference** tab is for looking things up. No stories, just the facts in tables.

## Pages marked TODO

A page with a **TODO** tag in the menu is not written yet. It gets written the day its process is tested and passes. The test list lives in Linear, in the project *Walk the whole business*.
