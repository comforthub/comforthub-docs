Stripe bills plan customers by the month and is the source of truth for a subscription's plan, amount and status. Nothing else is sold through it: invoices for jobs are paid on the pay page or in QuickBooks.

## What Stripe owns

| Thing | Where it lives | Who changes it |
|---|---|---|
| The customer's card and the monthly charge | Stripe | The customer, or the office in the Stripe dashboard |
| The plan and its price | A Stripe product and price | The office in Stripe |
| The subscription's status (Active, Past Due, Paused, Canceled) | Stripe | Stripe, on payment or cancellation |

Our side and Zoho hold copies. The engine's plan table maps each Stripe product to a plan name (All-Season, Heating Only, Cooling Only, Tankless) and the visits it includes. A product missing from that table is refused and flagged.

## The events we listen to

| Stripe event | What the engine does |
|---|---|
| Subscription created or updated | Creates or updates our subscription and the Zoho Subscription |
| Subscription deleted | Marks both Canceled |
| Invoice paid | Adds a subscription payment here and a Subscription Payment in Zoho, then refreshes the Subscription's Months Paid, Paid This Term and Last Payment Date |
| Invoice payment failed | Recorded only. The subscription update Stripe sends next carries the Past Due status |

Every call is checked against Stripe's signature and against a five-minute clock window, and an event seen before is ignored. Timings as observed are on [Subscriptions](/handbook/payments/subscriptions).

## Keys and mode

The account has one live secret key and the subscription webhook's signing secret, both in the secrets store under the functions path (see [Environments and secrets](/systems/environments-and-secrets)). There is no test-mode key, so Stripe's own side is tested with a real card, and the engine's side with the replay harness described on [Subscriptions](/handbook/payments/subscriptions).
