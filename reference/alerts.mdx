Every alert is an email to the operator with a subject that starts with **[ops]**. Each watch runs on its own clock (see [Scheduled jobs](/reference/scheduled-jobs)), and a finding is emailed once: the same trouble does not page again until it clears and comes back. What to do when one arrives is on [When an alert email arrives](/handbook/running/when-an-alert-email-arrives).

| Alert | Subject looks like | Fires when | Seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dead letter | [ops] (queue name) holds 2 dead letters | a message died in a queue after its retries | 3 Sep 2026 |
| Dead letter, already cleared | [ops] a message died in (queue name) and was already cleared | a message died and was redriven or dropped before the watch ran | 3 Sep 2026 |
| Dead letter, from the worker | [ops] (system) (operation) died: (reason in words) | the worker itself gives up on a message; one email per dead letter, sent at once | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Human review | [ops] 38 events await a person | an integration event is flagged for a person to look at; sent again only when the count changes. A refused Zoho login refresh raises one flag per spell (the 4 Sep 2026 spell raised 36, one per retry, before the fix; they were closed through the review route on 5 Sep, 104 open flags becoming 68) | 5 Sep 2026 |
| Queue health | [ops] 1 queue stalled, oldest 16m | a message on a live queue has waited more than 15 minutes for a worker | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Wake failure | [ops] 6 wake-up posts did not arrive | five or more wake-up posts failed in 15 minutes; one lost post is normal and stays quiet. The wake of the alert watches themselves waits a minute instead of 5 seconds, so an alert run of 8 seconds is no longer counted as a lost post (watched 5 Sep 2026 00:05 UTC, <Linear issue="COM-202" />); a worker wake that takes over 5 seconds still is, on purpose | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Cron failure | [ops] 3 scheduled jobs failing | three or more different timed jobs failed in 15 minutes; one job failing once is that job, not the machinery | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Orphan action | [ops] 1 queued action no drain owns | a queued action name no worker owns; it is put back on the queue each minute and would sit there forever | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Delete refused or ignored | [ops] 2 CRM deletes were ignored (1 invoice, 1 subscription payment) | a Zoho delete the sync rules refused (something still references the row) or ignored (the row is money); one digest per run with every record in the body, and "a CRM delete of a payment was ignored" when there is only one | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Sweep stale | [ops] the reconciliation sweep has not run in 4 hours | the nightly drift sweep has recorded nothing for 48 hours (seen with the threshold tightened to 1 hour for the test) | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Sweep watch blind | [ops] the sweep staleness watch has gone blind | the sweep watch itself cannot read the sweep log | not yet |
| Cancelled but collectible | [ops] INV… is still collectible and its work order is cancelled | a cancelled job still has money owed in QuickBooks | not yet |
| Cancelled after payment | [ops] INV008348 was PAID and its work order is cancelled | a job was cancelled after its invoice was paid; the automatic void refuses a paid invoice, so this is a refund decision | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Card number stored | [ops] a card number is stored in (the column) | a card number reached a text or a transcript; sent once per row until the row is cleaned | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Imagery missing | [ops] 1 property has no street-view image | a property with an address has waited a day for its image | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Invoice amounts | [ops] 3 invoices disagree across Postgres, QuickBooks and Zoho | the nightly amounts check finds an invoice whose total differs by more than two cents from QuickBooks, on our side or in Zoho, or is missing in QuickBooks; one finding per invoice, filed against QuickBooks, open until the amounts agree | 3 Sep 2026 |
| Payroll runway | [ops] the payroll calendar runs out in 111 days | fewer than 60 days of pay periods are loaded (seen with the threshold widened to 200 days for the test) | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Timesheets off calendar | [ops] 2 timesheets sit outside the payroll calendar | a timesheet's dates match no pay period | 26 Aug 2026 |

"Seen" is the last day the alert was watched landing in the operator's inbox during the walk.

## Invoice amounts: what a finding looks like

Two checks watch invoice totals. The nightly amounts check (early morning) compares every live invoice against QuickBooks and Zoho and emails the alert above. The reconciliation sweep (also nightly, and runnable by hand from the repo's Actions page) compares each Zoho record field by field and records what differs as a finding; it does not email.

Watched 4 September 2026 on a probe invoice of $5.65: with all three sides agreeing, the sweep recorded nothing for it. The total on our side was changed to $6.65 on purpose; the next sweep, 90 seconds later, opened one finding naming the invoice, the field, our value and Zoho's. The total was put back; the sweep after that closed the finding as "not re-seen". The morning alert email was not watched that day because it had already run and runs at most once in 20 hours.

Until 4 September 2026 the morning check filed its Zoho comparison under the sweep's name, and the sweep, which never looks for a QuickBooks id, closed those findings the same morning as "not re-seen"; three real invoices (INV008137, INV008200, INV008224) arrived as new every day for a week that way (<Linear issue="COM-193" />). Since the evening of 4 September the check files one finding per invoice against QuickBooks, naming whichever of our total and Zoho's disagrees, and the sweep leaves those alone: the QuickBooks findings of 31 August lived through 3 and 8 sweeps until they were settled. Watched 5 September 2026 at 00:05 UTC with the check run by hand: 93 invoices compared, none disagreed, no finding filed. The three invoices had been settled that morning (one voided, two brought into line in Zoho), so a disagreement was not seen landing in production that day; the local engine's test covers it.

The sweep has a ceiling: the nightly run fails if it records more than 160 findings, and a hand run from the Actions page takes the ceiling as an input. On 5 September 2026 at 00:11 UTC a hand run recorded 1,301 findings, up from 175 the evening before, almost all on fields added to the sync that day whose older records were never brought into line; the run failed its ceiling and the nightly one will until that is settled (<Linear issue="COM-211" />).

The cancelled-but-collectible alert has only been seen on the local engine (the stack test), never in production; the cancelled-after-payment one landed in production on 4 September 2026 when a paid probe job was cancelled in Zoho (the cancel reached us 1 minute 53 seconds later, the void was refused as settled the same second, and the email went out on the next wake). The card-number alert fired once, on 4 September 2026, against a card planted in a probe transcript line for the test; nothing real has tripped it.
