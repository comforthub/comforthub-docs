Every customer email goes through one sender that checks the address first: only our own addresses and our alias domain are delivered to as written; anything else is redirected to the operator with "[TEST → …]" in the subject. See [Email safety](/handbook/running/email-safety).

| Email | Subject | To | When | Attached | Seen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Booking confirmation | Your Comfort Hub appointment on … | customer | appointment booked with Notify Client on | none; **Add to calendar** link | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Appointment moved | Your Comfort Hub appointment has moved to … | customer | date changed | none; **Update your calendar** link | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Invoice | Invoice INV… from Comfort Hub | customer | invoice reaches QuickBooks | invoice PDF; pay link | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Receipt | Payment received for INV… | customer | invoice fully paid | receipt PDF | 4 Sep 2026 |
| Quote | Your Comfort Hub quote for WO… | customer | technician sends a quote | none; approve button, good for 30 days | 3 Sep 2026 |
| Stocking order | Purchase order PO… from Comfort Hub | supplier branch email; reply-to is the technician who saved it | a stocking order is saved with its PDF | purchase order PDF named after the number | 4 Sep 2026 |
| App login invite | Your Comfort Hub app login | technician | a technician in Zoho gets an email | none; one-time set-password button | 3 Sep 2026 |
| Dead-letter alert | [ops] a message died in … | operator | a message dies in a queue | none | 3 Sep 2026 |

## The calendar link

The two appointment emails carry a signed link that downloads a calendar file for the visit. Checked 4 September 2026: the file held the right day, the start of the arrival window in Ottawa time (10:00 for a "10:00-12:00 PM" window), the two-hour window, the property's street and city as the location, and the job's lines as the description. The link is good for 30 days or until the day after the visit, whichever is later; a link with a tampered signature is refused. Because the entry keeps one identity per appointment, adding the moved email's link replaces the first entry instead of making a second.
