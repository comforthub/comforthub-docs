Every customer email goes through one sender that checks the address first: only our own addresses and our alias domain are delivered to as written; anything else is redirected to the operator with "[TEST → …]" in the subject. See [Email safety](/handbook/running/email-safety).

| Email | Subject | To | When | Attached | Seen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Booking confirmation | Your Comfort Hub appointment on … | customer | appointment booked with Notify Client on | none; add-to-calendar link | 3 Sep 2026 |
| Appointment moved | Your Comfort Hub appointment has moved to … | customer | date changed | none | 3 Sep 2026 |
| Invoice | Invoice INV… from Comfort Hub | customer | invoice reaches QuickBooks | invoice PDF; pay link | 3 Sep 2026 |
| Receipt | Payment received for INV… | customer | invoice fully paid | receipt PDF | 3 Sep 2026 |
| Quote | Your Comfort Hub quote for WO… | customer | technician sends a quote | none; approve button, good for 30 days | 3 Sep 2026 |
| App login invite | Your Comfort Hub app login | technician | a technician in Zoho gets an email | none; one-time set-password button | 3 Sep 2026 |
| Dead-letter alert | [ops] a message died in … | operator | a message dies in a queue | none | 3 Sep 2026 |

Not sent today: an appointment reminder the day before, and invoice reminders.
