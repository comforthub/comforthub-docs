Times are Ottawa time (Eastern). The engine keeps them in UTC, so they shift by an hour when the clocks change.

## Every minute

| Job | What it does |
|---|---|
| Wake the sync worker | Nudges the worker that carries changes between Zoho and Supabase, in case a queue has work |
| Wake the action worker | Nudges the worker that does everything else: QuickBooks, emails, PDFs, pushes, invites |

## Watches that send an alert

Each runs on its own clock, looks for one kind of trouble, and emails the operator once per finding. See [Alerts](/reference/alerts) and [When an alert email arrives](/handbook/running/when-an-alert-email-arrives).

| Job | When | Looks for |
|---|---|---|
| Dead-letter watch | every 15 minutes | a message that died in a queue |
| Human-review watch | every 15 minutes | an event waiting for a person |
| Delete-refused watch | every 15 minutes | a Zoho delete the rules refused |
| Queue-health watch | every 15 minutes | a message on a live queue that nobody has picked up for 15 minutes |
| Wake-failure watch | every 15 minutes | five or more wake-up calls in 15 minutes that did not reach their function |
| Cron-failure watch | no timer of its own; runs whenever any other watch wakes the alert function | three or more different timed jobs failing in 15 minutes |
| Orphan-action watch | 20 past the hour | a queued action no worker owns |
| Sweep watch | 25 past the hour | a nightly drift sweep that has not run |
| Cancelled-but-collectible watch | 35 past the hour | a cancelled job whose invoice is still owed in QuickBooks |
| Card-number scan | 45 past the hour | a card number in a text or a transcript |
| Imagery watch | 50 past the hour | a property still without its Street View image |
| Invoice-amounts check | 1:10 am | an invoice whose amount differs between Supabase, QuickBooks and Zoho |
| Payroll-calendar check | 2:55 am | fewer than 60 days of pay periods left |

## Nightly clean-ups

| Job | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Prune cron history | 11:25 pm | Drops old cron run records |
| Prune queue archives | 11:35 pm | Drops old archived queue messages |
| Close the call sync window | 12:17 am | Stops syncing calls older than 30 days |
| Prune integration history | 12:30 am | Drops old integration events past their keep window |
| Sweep property imagery | 1:40 am | Fetches the Street View image for any property still missing one |

## Reminders and pushes

| Job | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Open-appointment push | every hour | Looks for appointments still open an hour past their window. Each one gets a technician one push the first hour it is overdue, then one a day at 8:00 am until it is completed, not one every hour (seen 3 and 4 September 2026: a push at 11:00 am, then the next at 8:00 am the day after and nothing at the twenty hours between) |
| Auto-submit overdue timesheets | 1:00 am | Submits any timesheet still open after its deadline |
| Timesheet reminder, morning | 9:00 am | Push to anyone whose timesheet is due |
| Tasks due soon | 9:00 am | Push for tasks due today |
| Timesheet reminder, evening | 7:00 pm | Second push to anyone whose timesheet is still open |

## Digests

|---|---|---|

## The nightly drift sweep

The sweep itself runs from GitHub, not from this list. It compares every synced record on both sides and opens a finding for each difference, and closes a finding the next time the two sides agree. See [How sync works](/handbook/running/how-sync-works).

- Scheduled at 2:20 am (6:20 UTC). GitHub runs scheduled jobs late when it is busy: the last four nightly runs started at 7:40, 7:19, 7:15 and 7:19 am, about five hours after the hour they were asked for. The sweep watch tolerates 48 hours, so the delay does not page.
- One run takes about a minute. On 4 September 2026 a run by hand started at 9:12 am and had written its findings by 9:13, and the same run is what the sweep watch reads.
- The reconciler, which copies Zoho's value over ours for an open finding, has no schedule. A person runs it from GitHub, and it prints what it would write without writing unless told otherwise. On 4 September 2026 a dry run at 17:48 UTC listed 58 planned writes, all on fields Zoho owns, and wrote nothing; the links it measured were listed separately as never written.
- The nightly run goes red when the open findings exceed 160. The findings are still written; the red is the gauge. How to run both by hand is on [Run the sweep and the reconciler](/handbook/running/run-the-sweep-and-the-reconciler).

The cron-failure watch above has no timer of its own on purpose: if the timed jobs are failing, a timer is the wrong thing to rely on. It rides on every other wake instead, which means it is quiet when every other watch is quiet too.
