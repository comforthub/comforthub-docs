When the work is done, the technician writes the note and taps **Complete** twice. That one action closes the appointment and the work order on both sides, stamps who and when, and starts billing.

## Before you start

- Every required ring on the work order done: checklists answered, serials and photos where the job asks for them.
- The completion note written.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Complete">
    On the work order, tap **Complete**. The app shows what is still open, if anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap **Complete** again. The appointment is marked complete with the time, and the work order is marked complete with your name and your note.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- In Zoho, the appointment and the work order show Completed, with Completed By and Completed At.
- Completed At on the work order is the moment the last checklist was finished, not the moment of the tap, so it matches what the customer saw.
- Seen 3 September 2026: one second after completing probe job WO461, Zoho showed both records Completed with the person and the time, and the invoice step began in the same second.
- Seen again 4 September 2026 on a probe job completed straight on our side, the way the app's tap lands: the appointment was Completed in Zoho 2 seconds later with the tap time; the work order was Completed in Zoho with Jeremy as **Completed By**, the note, and **Completed At** equal to the checklist's finish time (13:20, not the 13:29 tap); its **Billing Status** read Invoiced 11 seconds after the tap and the invoice email left at 15 seconds. The proof of the tap itself in the app is a separate test.

## What happens next

Billing runs on its own: see [How invoicing works](/handbook/invoicing/how-invoicing-works). A job with only warranty or plan lines is marked Non-Billable and stops there.

## If the job was completed in Zoho instead

The status arrives here and the time is stamped, but that stamp does not go back to Zoho today (tracked as COM-176). Complete from the app when you can.
