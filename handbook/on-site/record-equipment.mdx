## Equipment made when an install completes

You do not add the furnace or condenser an install puts in by hand. When the work order is completed, each line whose product converts to an asset becomes a piece of equipment on the customer, whether the job was booked from the dispatch widget (which makes it at booking time) or typed straight into Zoho.

Tested 4 September 2026 on a probe install typed into Zoho with one furnace line: completed at 23:43:54 UTC, the furnace was on our side in the same instant, in Zoho Assets 3 seconds later with its brand, type, account and property, and on the work order's **Work Order Assets** list 5 seconds later. It had no serial yet; the serial and photos are what you record on site.
