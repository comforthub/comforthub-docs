When a technician finds more work than the job was booked for, they build a quote in the app and send it. The customer gets an email with an **Approve** button. Tapping it adds the quoted lines to the work order, tells the technician, and carries the lines to Zoho. Nobody in the office has to touch it.

## Before you start

- The work order open in the app, on the day of the visit.
- The parts and labour you want to quote. Catalog items pull their price; a part that is not in the catalog can be typed in with its own price.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the quote">
    On the work order, open **Actions → Quote**. Add the lines with quantity and price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Tap **Send**. The customer gets "Your Comfort Hub quote for WO…" with the lines, the tax and the total, and an Approve button. The button works for 30 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The customer approves">
    They tap Approve and see "Thank you, your quote is approved". Tapping it again shows "Already approved" and changes nothing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- The technician gets a push: "&lt;customer&gt; approved your quote", with the total, that opens the work order.
- The quoted lines are on the work order, charged to the customer and marked as added by Quote. The original lines are untouched.
- The same lines are on the work order in Zoho within a few seconds, and the billing decision runs again.
- Tested 3 September 2026 on a probe job: the email left in under a second, the approval added two lines and the push reached every one of the technician's devices in one second, and Zoho had both lines seven seconds after the tap.

## If something goes wrong

- **"That link is not right"** means the link was cut short or was for another quote. Send the quote again.
- **"This link has expired"** means more than 30 days went by. Send the quote again; the new email has a fresh link.
- **The customer has no email** on their record: the quote is saved but nothing is sent, and the skip is logged. Add the email in Zoho and send again.
- The name on a quoted line that matches a catalog item is replaced by the catalog description when it lands on the job. That is a known wrinkle, tracked as COM-174.
