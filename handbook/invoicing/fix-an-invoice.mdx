Small fixes are made on the Zoho invoice. The engine carries them to our side and to QuickBooks.

## Before you start

- The invoice open in Zoho, not yet paid.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change the discount or the due date">
    Edit **Discount** or **Due Date** and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Tested 3 September 2026: a $1 discount reached our side in 2 seconds and QuickBooks 2 seconds later, shown as a discount line; the total went from $5.65 to $4.52 in all three places.

## What you cannot change

- The amount. It is computed from the lines. Change the lines on the work order instead, before the job is completed.
- The invoice number and the payment state. Those are ours and QuickBooks'.
