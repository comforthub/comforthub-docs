An invoice is voided from Zoho. The engine voids it in QuickBooks and files a VOID copy of the PDF.

## Before you start

- The invoice open in Zoho, unpaid. A paid invoice is not voided this way.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the status">
    Set **Status** to **Void** and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Tested 3 September 2026 on INV008344: our side was Void in 2 seconds, QuickBooks voided the invoice 1 second later, and the VOID PDF was stored 6 seconds after that.
- The pay page for that invoice shows it as void and offers no pay button.

## Good to know

- Cancelling an invoiced work order voids its invoice the same way.
- The work order keeps saying Invoiced after a void. Known.
