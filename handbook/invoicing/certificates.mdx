A certificate is a PDF the engine writes when a maintenance or commissioning job is completed. It lists the equipment and the readings the technician took.

| Job type | Certificate | Where it goes | Tested |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maintenance | Certificate of Maintenance | stored on the work order; attached to the Zoho work order | 3 Sep 2026, 9 s after completion |
| Commissioning | Certificate of Commissioning | same | not yet |
| Service, installation, warranty | none | | |

The certificate is not sent with the invoice email today; that email carries the invoice PDF only. Readings come from the completed checklist; a checklist completed without readings gives a certificate with none.
