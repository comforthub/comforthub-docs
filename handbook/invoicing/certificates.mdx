title: "Certificates"
description: "Which visits produce a certificate, where it is stored, and who gets it"
kind: reference
---

A certificate is a PDF the engine writes when a job is completed, one per visit whose checklist earned it. The checklist template's category decides which certificate, not the job type.

| Checklist category | Certificate | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance | Certificate of Maintenance | filed by the engine; attached to the Zoho work order; attached to the invoice email next to the invoice PDF |
| Commissioning | Certificate of Commissioning | the same |
| Any other category, or two categories on one visit | none | |

A service job never produces a certificate, whatever its checklists. On every other kind of job, each completed visit with at least one finished checklist gets one certificate when all its checklists share one of the two categories. The file is named after the certificate kind, the job and the visit date.

The invoice email waits for every certificate the job earned before it goes out, and attaches each one.
