A payroll record flows both ways: the name is shared, Zoho owns the processing status and summary, and a delete in Zoho removes the row here. One of Zoho's four processing statuses, **Needs Review**, is refused by our side today, and a payroll set to it in Zoho dead-letters instead of landing.

## What was done

Test <Linear issue="COM-150" />, run in production on 4 September 2026 with a record made in Zoho for the test: **Probe COM-150 Payroll** (Zoho record 5665332000031477025, our row 2adfa3fa-b26d-4b5f-b819-9973e6148209).

1. Made in Zoho at 13:58:32 UTC.
2. Edited in Zoho (status **Processing**, summary **Probe COM-150 v1**) just before 14:00.
3. Renamed on our side to **Probe COM-150 Payroll (pg edit)** at 14:01:31.
4. Status set in Zoho to **Needs Review** at 17:43:40, to test the one value the first step had pointed at.
5. Deleted in Zoho at 17:50:50.

## What was seen

| Step | In Zoho | On our side | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Made in Zoho | 13:58:32 | 13:58:34.6, **refused**: the status broke a rule on the table, and the notice went to the dead-letter queue | 2 s |
| Edited in Zoho | just before 14:00 | 14:00:24.3, row created with status **processing** | Zoho held the notice; our side applied it in 0.6 s |
| Edited on our side | 14:01:31.4, Zoho showed the new name | 14:01:31 | pushed in under a second |
| Set to Needs Review in Zoho | 17:43:40 | 17:44:49.8, **refused** the same way, and a dead-letter email left at 17:44:49 | 70 s |
| Deleted in Zoho | 17:50:50 | 17:53:36.0, row removed | 2¾ min, of which 0.1 s ours |

The push at 14:01 came back as one notice at 14:01:45, applied once and not pushed again.

## What to know

- **Needs Review cannot land here yet.** Zoho offers Processing, Completed, Failed and Needs Review; our side lowercases whichever arrives and accepts only the first three, so a payroll set to Needs Review in Zoho is refused, the notice is dead-lettered, and the ops mailbox gets a dead-letter email at once, saying the payroll apply died of invalid data. Every other edit on that record still lands. The fix (accept all four) is on a branch in pull request 597 and is not live at the time of writing. Until it is, do not use Needs Review in Zoho, and if one dead-letters, set the status back and redrive it; see [Redrive a dead letter](/handbook/running/redrive-a-dead-letter).
- **Title case is not the problem.** Processing typed in Zoho lands as processing here, seen at 14:00:24. The morning's first refusal most likely carried Needs Review at creation; what the record held at 13:58 was not recorded anywhere, so that part is unverified.
- The 13:58 dead letter and the 17:44 one were both archived by hand the same day (the second at 17:51, by someone else). The probe never carried a pay period or a schedule file, so nothing else was exercised.
