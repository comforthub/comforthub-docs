An assigned checklist flows both ways: Zoho owns its type and description, the name is shared, and a delete in Zoho removes the row here.

## What was done

Test <Linear issue="COM-146" />, run in production on 4 September 2026 with a record made for the test: **Probe COM-146 Assigned Checklist** (Zoho record 5665332000031478051, our row 5c2e9a37-9de7-4f59-94dd-fae9ad88ad83), type Maintenance.

1. Made in Zoho at 13:58:25 UTC.
2. Description changed in Zoho to **Probe COM-146 v2 (zoho edit)** just before 14:00.
3. Renamed on our side to **Probe COM-146 Assigned Checklist (pg edit)** at 14:01:31.
4. Deleted in Zoho at 17:50:50.

## What was seen

| Step | In Zoho | On our side | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Made in Zoho | 13:58:25 | 13:58:29.0 | 4 s, of which 0.6 s ours |
| Edited in Zoho | just before 14:00 | 14:00:17.5 | Zoho held the notice; our side applied it in 0.6 s |
| Edited on our side | 14:01:31.4, Zoho showed the new name | 14:01:31 | pushed in under a second |
| Deleted in Zoho | 17:50:50 | 17:53:24.5, row removed | 2½ min, all but 2.8 s of it Zoho holding the notice |

Our push came back from Zoho as one notice at 14:01:45, applied as an update and not pushed again: a change does not bounce.

## What to know

- The type and the description are Zoho's. A value typed here for either is not sent up; the name is the one field both sides may write.
- On delete the row is removed outright and its link to the Zoho record is tombstoned, so a later notice about the same record cannot bring it back.
