A checklist submission is normally made by the app when a technician answers a checklist, and it reaches Zoho from here. Made in Zoho by hand, it lands here too, and the one field both sides write, the submitted time, flows both ways.

## What was done

Test <Linear issue="COM-147" />, run in production on 4 September 2026 with a submission typed into Zoho for the test: **Probe COM-147 Submission** (Zoho record 5665332000031456029, our row d543e97f-84f9-4475-a1d4-7afc1d209c8b), on the probe template of <Linear issue="COM-145" />, the appointment **Probe COM-35 Appointment**, and the probe technician's app user.

1. Made in Zoho at 14:01:25 UTC.
2. Submitted time changed in Zoho to 14:05 at 17:43:38.
3. Submitted time changed on our side to 14:10 at 17:45:59.
4. Deleted in Zoho at 17:50:50.

## What was seen

| Step | In Zoho | On our side | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Made in Zoho | 14:01:25 | 14:01:28.1 | 3 s, of which 0.9 s ours |
| Edited in Zoho | 17:43:38 | 17:44:48.1 | 70 s, of which 0.5 s ours |
| Edited on our side | 17:46:01.7, Zoho showed 14:10 | 17:45:59.3 | pushed in 2.4 s |
| Deleted in Zoho | 17:50:50 | 17:53:25.2, row removed | 2½ min, of which 0.2 s ours |

The push came back as one notice at 17:47:59 and was applied once, with no second push. The appointment the submission named was untouched by the delete.

## What to know

- A submission typed into Zoho has no answers. It lands here as a pending checklist with a completed time and nothing else; the real path is the app, see [Answer a checklist](/handbook/on-site/answer-a-checklist).
- Which template and which appointment it belongs to are Zoho's to set; our side reads them and does not send them up.
- Delete the submissions of a template before the template, or the template delete is refused; see [How checklist templates sync](/handbook/sync-checks/checklist-templates).
