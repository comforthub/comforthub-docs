A timesheet travels with its entries. Our side owns the period, the hours, the status and the submitted time; Zoho owns the approval time and the rejection reason; a delete in Zoho removes the timesheet and its entries here.

## What was done

Test <Linear issue="COM-148" />, run in production on 4 September 2026 with a timesheet made in Zoho for the test: **Probe COM-148 Timesheet** (Zoho record 5665332000031465021, our row 27474e4d-9886-4498-b52c-663f0fa85e07), pay period 20 of 2026, one entry of 6 hours on 8 September, on the probe technician's app user (not a real technician).

1. Made in Zoho at 13:58:57 UTC.
2. Rejection reason set in Zoho to **Probe COM-148 zoho edit** just before 14:00.
3. Edited on our side at 14:01:32.
4. Deleted in Zoho at 17:50:50.

## What was seen

| Step | In Zoho | On our side | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Made in Zoho | 13:58:57 | 13:59:03.9 | 7 s, of which 0.7 s ours; the entry arrived with it |
| Edited in Zoho | just before 14:00 | 14:00:18.7 | Zoho held the notice; our side applied it in 0.9 s |
| Edited on our side | 14:01:32.8 and 14:01:35.0, two pushes | 14:01:32 | in Zoho within 3 s, the entry with it |
| Deleted in Zoho | 17:50:50 | 17:53:28.0, timesheet and entry removed | 2½ min, of which 0.3 s ours |

The pushes came back as one notice at 14:01:44, applied once and not pushed again. The technician's app user was untouched by the delete.

## What to know

- A timesheet typed into Zoho must name an app user in its **Supabase User Id** field; without one it cannot be created here.
- The status is a state machine on our side (draft, submitted, approved, rejected). A status typed in Zoho is not applied here; approve or reject in the app or through the approval time and rejection reason, which Zoho does own. See [Approve timesheets and time off](/handbook/hours/approve-timesheets-and-time-off).
- An edit on our side re-sends the whole timesheet with all its entries, so a one-field change shows as two pushes a couple of seconds apart.
