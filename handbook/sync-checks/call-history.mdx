Calls are made by the phone system: every Twilio call leg becomes a row here and a record in Zoho, in about a second. Typed into Zoho by hand, a call lands here too, and its direction flows both ways.

## What was done

Test <Linear issue="COM-151" />, run in production on 4 September 2026 with a call typed into Zoho for the test: **Probe COM-151 Call** (Zoho record 5665332000031455023, our row d421b303-5fab-4822-b173-e732c716eca8), inbound, 17:00 to 17:05 UTC, department Other matters.

1. Made in Zoho at 17:43:57 UTC.
2. Call type changed in Zoho to **Outbound** at 17:46:59.
3. Direction changed on our side back to inbound at 17:49:49.
4. Deleted in Zoho at 17:54:58.

## What was seen

| Step | In Zoho | On our side | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Made in Zoho | 17:43:57 | 17:45:31.3, row created | 94 s, of which 1.6 s ours |
| Edited in Zoho | 17:46:59 | 17:48:52.4, direction outbound | 113 s, of which 0.7 s ours |
| Edited on our side | 17:49:50.6, Zoho showed **Inbound** | 17:49:48.8 | pushed in 1.8 s |
| Deleted in Zoho | 17:54:58 | 17:55:25.5, row removed | 27 s, of which 0.1 s ours |

The push came back as one notice at 17:51:52, applied once and not pushed again. The five real Twilio calls of the day (13:11 to 17:16) were each in Zoho within 4 seconds of landing here, and Zoho's echo of each came back 18 to 85 seconds later as one apply.

## What to know

- **The call log was reset on 5 September 2026.** Every call record on both sides (999 in Zoho, 999 here, plus recordings, transcript segments, voicemails and callback requests) was deleted so the log starts fresh with Twilio only. The two Zoom-era fields on the Zoho module, the Zoom call id and the old Supabase call id, were deleted the same day; the Supabase call reference is the key the sync uses. The two Zoom call-log tables were exported by the owner and removed on 5 September 2026, together with the 234 old call classifications that pointed at them, which were not exported.

- A call typed into Zoho is a thin row here: direction and start and end times, a made-up call id, the primary phone line, and a status of **queued** that nothing will ever move on. Only the direction and the two times are mirrored; the department, the result and the transcript fields are Zoho's alone. Nothing in the phone flow reads such a row, so it is harmless, but it is not a call.
- Zoho was slow that afternoon: 94 to 113 seconds to send word of a create or an edit, against 25 to 130 seconds seen on other modules the same day. Our side took under 2 seconds each time.
