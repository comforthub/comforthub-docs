While a record is locked in Zoho, changes still flow down to our side, but a change made here is not sent up: the push is skipped, an event says why, the event is flagged for a person, and the next review email counts it. When the lock record for that record is resolved or removed, the refused change is sent once more.

## What was done

Test <Linear issue="COM-155" />, run in production on 4 September 2026 with an account made in Zoho for the test: **Probe COM-155 Account** (Zoho record 5665332000031470055, our row d6d9cb1a-d349-4f3f-bf1b-4c8dffd50740). Accounts, customers, properties and equipment have Zoho record locking switched on, by rule and by hand; assigned checklists, work orders and leads do not.

1. Made in Zoho at 17:48:38 UTC; here at 17:50:29.
2. Locked in Zoho at 17:51:07 through Zoho's record-lock action (reason **Probe COM-155 lock test**). Zoho then showed the record as locked and not editable.
3. Renamed on our side to **Probe COM-155 Account (pg edit while locked)** at 17:51:32.
4. Unlocked in Zoho at 18:00:30, the flag closed with a note at 18:00:51, and the account deleted in Zoho at 18:01.

## What was seen

| Step | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| Edit on our side | 17:51:32.3 | the row changed and a push was queued |
| Push refused | 17:51:33.7 | the push was skipped with the reason "record locked in the CRM", and the event was flagged for a person |
| Review email | 18:00:06 | the quarter-hour wake mailed "[ops] 43 events await a person", one more than the 17:50 count; the refusal is in it as one line of the breakdown by operation, not by record |
| Unlocked in Zoho | 18:00:30 | Zoho still said **Probe COM-155 Account** while our side said **Probe COM-155 Account (pg edit while locked)**; nothing was queued to send it, so the two sides stayed apart |
| Deleted in Zoho | 18:01:10.6 | the row here went inactive, as an account does, 11 s after the delete and 0.2 s after Zoho's notice |

## What to know

- **It is not silent, but it is not loud either.** The refusal is one flagged event, and the review email that goes out every fifteen minutes counts it with every other open flag ("N events await a person") and names it by operation. Nothing lists which records are locked, and nothing mails at the moment of refusal.
- **The change catches up when the lock record lifts.** Once refused, the push is archived. When the lock record naming that record (see [How record locks sync](/handbook/sync-checks/record-locks)) is resolved or deleted, every change refused while it was locked is queued once more and sent within the minute, carrying the record's current state; if Zoho still says locked, it is refused and flagged again. This ships in pull request 621 (test COM-215); the test above ran before it, which is why the rename never landed.
- **A bare unlock is still silent.** Zoho sends no notice for a lock or an unlock of the record itself. A record locked and unlocked with Zoho's own lock action alone, with no lock record made, is not re-sent; the next push of that row, whenever something else on it changes, carries the edit. Until then the two sides disagree on that field, and the nightly reconcile is the only thing that would notice (not exercised here).
- **The flag stays for a person.** Re-sending does not close it: closing it is [Resolve a human-review flag](/handbook/running/resolve-a-human-review-flag). Ten earlier refusals, all leads on 31 August, sit in review the same way.
