A checklist template is a list of readings a technician takes for one kind of equipment: supply air temperature, static pressure, and so on. It is written in Zoho, in **Checklist Templates**, and the app reads the copy on our side.

## Before you start

- Zoho open, on **Checklist Templates**.
- The equipment type it is for, and the readings with their units and the band each should fall in.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the template">
    In **Checklist Templates**, click **Create**. Fill in **Name**, pick a **Category** (Maintenance, Commissioning, Post-repair or Project Scope) and the **Asset Type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the questions">
    In **Checklist Questions**, add one row per reading: **Question Title**, **Unit**, **Expected Min** and **Expected Max**, or **Nominal** with **Tolerance Pct**. Set **Display Order** and tick **Required** where it matters. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the engine suggest questions (optional)">
    The **Generate questions** button on the record asks the engine for readings suited to the equipment type. It answered a furnace prompt with four grounded readings in the test. Keep the ones you want and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Tested 3 September 2026: the template and its three questions were on our side in 2 seconds, in order, with units and bands, without pressing any button.

## Good to know

- Only readings exist. There are no yes/no, text or choice questions in the Zoho form.
- Zoho is the owner. Edit questions there, never on our side.
