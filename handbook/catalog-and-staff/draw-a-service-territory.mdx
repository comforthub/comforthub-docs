A territory is two things: a record in Zoho with a name, a code and an active flag, and a map area that lives only on our side. You create the record; a developer draws the area. From then on every new property whose address falls inside the area gets that territory on both sides.

## Before you start

- Zoho open, on **Service Territories**.
- A short code for the territory in the same shape as the others (for example TERR-EAST-003).
- The rough map area agreed, so the developer knows what to draw.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the record">
    In **Service Territories**, click **Create**. Fill in **Name** and **Major Service Area** (the code), tick **Is Active**, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for the map area">
    Zoho has no map. Ask a developer to draw the area on our side; that is a database change with the territory's code. Until it is drawn, the territory exists but no property falls into it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a property">
    Add or convert a customer whose address sits inside the area. Open the property in Zoho: **Major Service Area** shows the new territory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

Tested 4 September 2026 with a probe territory whose area was drawn over Cornwall:

- The record was on our side 2 seconds after the sync picked it up, with name, code and active flag.
- A new property inside the area had its map position, its Street View picture and the territory within 4 seconds of being created, and Zoho showed the territory on the property 2 seconds after that.
- Switching **Is Active** off on our side reached Zoho in 2 seconds, and switching it back on took 2 seconds too. The active flag on all eight live territories reads the same in both places.

## Good to know

- The **Allowed Time Slots** you tick in Zoho are the arrival windows the territory offers here. Zoho owns them: a tick or a clear made in Zoho reached our side in 4 to 5 seconds on 4 September 2026, and nothing on our side changes them. Before that day the ticks did not reach us at all (<Linear issue="COM-194" />).
- Zoho's **Supabase Territory Id** stays empty on a territory you create in Zoho until our side first pushes an edit back; then it fills in. Either way the link is kept on our side.
- A property only gets its territory when its address can be placed on the map. An address the map does not know keeps no territory and no picture; fix the address and the property is tried again.
- Two overlapping areas: the property goes to the one whose code sorts first.
