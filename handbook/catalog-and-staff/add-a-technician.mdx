A technician is created in Zoho, in **Technicians**. That makes them bookable on the dispatch calendar. If you type their email, they are invited to the app on their own: no developer, no dashboard.

## Before you start

- Zoho open, on **Technicians**.
- The person's name, email and hourly rate.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the technician">
    In **Technicians**, click **Create**. Fill in **Name**, **Email**, **Hourly Rate**, and tick **Is Active**. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They get their login">
    Within a minute the technician has "Your Comfort Hub app login" in their inbox with a **Set my password** button. Tapping it on their phone opens the app and asks for a password. They sign in as a technician, tied to their Zoho record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Tested 3 September 2026: the technician was on our side in 2 seconds with name, email, rate and active flag, and could be put on an appointment. Their invite email left one second after the email was typed, and their app user existed with the technician role.
- Add the email later and the invite goes then. A technician who already has a login with that email is linked to it, not invited again. An inactive technician is never invited.
- The home location typed in Zoho is not carried over.
