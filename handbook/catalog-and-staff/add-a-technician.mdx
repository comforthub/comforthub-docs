A technician is created in Zoho, in **Technicians**. That makes them bookable on the dispatch calendar. Their app login is a separate step done by hand today.

## Before you start

- Zoho open, on **Technicians**.
- The person's name, email and hourly rate.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the technician">
    In **Technicians**, click **Create**. Fill in **Name**, **Email**, **Hourly Rate**, and tick **Is Active**. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give them an app login (by hand today)">
    Ask a developer to invite the email from the Supabase dashboard and set the role. Nothing does this on its own yet; see [Invite someone to the app](/handbook/app/invite-someone-to-the-app).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Tested 3 September 2026: the technician was on our side in 2 seconds with name, rate and active flag, and could be put on an appointment.
- The email and home location typed in Zoho are not carried over.
