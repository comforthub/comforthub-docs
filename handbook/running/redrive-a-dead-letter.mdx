A message that fails all its retries, or that the database refuses in a way a retry could never change, is moved to the queue's dead-letter box and the dead-letter alert tells you. Nothing is lost: the message is a pointer to a record, and once the cause is fixed it can be put back on the queue and run again, from the record's current state.

## Before you start

- The dead-letter alert, which names the queue and the message number.
- The cause fixed. A redrive with the cause still there just dies again.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the cause">
    The alert body and the flagged event say why in plain words. Fix that first: add the missing catalog item, correct the record, ship the code fix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the redrive">
    In the engine's GitHub repository, run the **redrive** workflow with mode **write**, the queue named in the alert, and the message number. It moves the message back onto its queue, and the worker picks it up within a minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the record">
    Look at the record the message pointed to. The outcome the message was meant to produce is now there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear any twins">
    If the same record died more than once (two triggers, two messages), redrive one and delete the others; the second run would only repeat the first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- The dead-letter box is empty and the next dead-letter watch stays quiet.
- Seen 3 September 2026: probe job WO461's invoice died twice (a typed-in part with no QuickBooks item). After the fix shipped, message 39 was redriven at 06:00 UTC and the invoice existed 3 seconds later, in QuickBooks and Zoho within 10 seconds, with the invoice email out at 06:00:04. Message 40, its twin, was deleted.

## Why some letters arrive at once

Two kinds of failure reach the box, and the alert body says which.

- **A refusal the database will repeat.** A value missing that the record needs, a value this side cannot read, a record colliding with one that already exists. Retrying would only get the same answer, so the message dies on its first delivery: one letter, one email and one flag for a person, within a minute of the record arriving. The alert reads "attempt 1 of 10, not retryable".
- **Trouble that passes.** Zoho or QuickBooks down or refusing to talk, the database briefly unreachable. The message is retried on a widening schedule, ten deliveries spread over ten to thirty minutes, and only then dies. The alert reads "attempt 10 of 10, budget spent".

Until 4 September 2026 both kinds took the long road: two probe leads made in Zoho that afternoon, one without a street and one with the phone and street of an existing lead, were each retried for eighteen minutes and left sixteen flags between them before their letters arrived. The first-delivery rule ships in pull request 619; before it, the rule covered only three kinds of refusal by name.
