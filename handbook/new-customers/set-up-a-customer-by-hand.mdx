Most customers come from a converted lead. When one does not, you can type the three records into Zoho yourself. Each one reaches our side in about 2 seconds, the contact gets a QuickBooks customer, and the property gets its picture and territory. What matters is the links between them, because our side copies those too.

## Before you start

- The person's name, email (lowercase) and phone.
- The service address, and the billing address if it differs.
- One account per customer. Our side allows exactly one, so a contact who already has an account cannot be given a second.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the contact">
    In **Contacts**, click **Create**, fill in the name, **Email** and **Phone**, and save. Leave **Is Active** ticked; it starts ticked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    In **Accounts**, click **Create**. Name it after the person, set **Contact Name** to the contact, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the property">
    In **Properties**, click **Create**. Fill in the address, set **Account** to the account, and save. Make a second property if billing differs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link them together">
    Open the contact. Set **Account Name** to the account, then **Service Address** and **Billing Address** to the properties. Do the same two address fields on the account. Save each.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The address fields only offer properties that belong to the contact's account, so the account has to be set first.

## How you know it worked

- The property shows a **Latitude**, a picture and a **Major Service Area** within a few seconds.
- The contact shows a **QBO Id**.
- In QuickBooks, the customer has the billing and shipping addresses. They appear a few seconds after you link the addresses on the contact, not before.

Tested 2 September 2026: all three records were on our side in 2 seconds each, linked to each other and active, and the QuickBooks customer had both addresses 4 seconds after the links were set.

## When the house sells

The buyer is a new contact. Create them as in step 1, then open the account and change **Contact Name** to the buyer. Our side follows in 2 seconds, and the same change made on our side reaches Zoho in 2 seconds. Tested the same day.

Do not point the account at a contact who already has one; our side refuses it and the change sits in the queue until you undo it.

## If it did not work

- **The contact or account shows as inactive on our side.** Someone unticked **Is Active**. Tick it and save.
- **QuickBooks has the customer but no address.** The contact's **Service Address** and **Billing Address** are not set. Set them and save.
