Converting is one field change. You set the lead's **Status** to **Converted** and save. The engine makes the Contact, the Account, the Property or Properties and the QuickBooks customer, and links them all. Do not use Zoho's own **Convert** button: the engine only watches **Status**, and what the button does to our side has not been tested.

## Before you start

- The lead has an email, a phone and a complete service address: street, city, province, postal code and country.
- If the customer is billed somewhere else, the billing address is filled in too. A different billing address makes a second property; a blank one does not.
- The email is lowercase.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the lead">
    In **Leads**, open the lead. Check the addresses one more time; they become the property records exactly as typed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status">
    Set **Status** to **Converted** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait a moment">
    Give it 15 seconds, then refresh the lead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- **Supabase Prospect Id** on the lead is filled in.
- In **Contacts** there is one contact with the lead's name. It shows a **QBO Id**, an **Account Name**, a **Service Address** and a **Billing Address**.
- In **Accounts** there is one account with the same name, linked to the contact and to the same addresses.
- In **Properties** there is one property per distinct address. Each shows a **Latitude** and **Longitude**, a picture of the house, and a **Major Service Area**.
- In QuickBooks there is one customer with that name.

Tested 2 September 2026: all of this was in place 15 seconds after Save.

## If it did not work

- **Nothing appeared after a minute.** The lead is probably missing an email or part of the service address. Fill it in, set **Status** to **New**, save, then set it to **Converted** again. The engine only runs on the change to **Converted**.
- **Something appeared but not all of it.** See [Fix a customer that did not set up properly](/handbook/new-customers/fix-a-customer-that-did-not-set-up).
- **The contact and account appeared, the property did not, and a dead-letter alert arrived.** Another customer already has a property at that exact street. Today the engine cannot make a second property at one address (COM-179). Until that is fixed, add the property to the account by hand in Zoho.

## Good to know

- Converting the same lead twice does nothing the second time. There will never be two contacts or two QuickBooks customers for one lead.
- The lead stays in **Leads** with **Status** Converted. It is not moved or deleted.
- From here on, edit the contact, account and property, not the lead. Changes to the lead after conversion do not reach the customer records.
