A prospect is a person who might become a customer. You add them in Zoho. The engine copies them over on its own; you never type them into Supabase.

## Before you start

- Zoho open, on the **Prospects** module.
- The person's name at minimum. Email and a service address are needed before they can be converted, so get them now if you can.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the record">
    In **Prospects**, click **Create**. Fill in **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and the service address fields (street, city, postal code). Set **Status** to **New** and **Source** to where they came from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick Active">
    Tick **Is Active**. Zoho leaves it unticked by default, and an unticked prospect arrives on our side marked inactive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. That is all. There is no sync button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Honestly, Zoho shows you nothing. The **Supabase Prospect Id** field stays blank for a prospect made in Zoho; it only fills in when the engine created the record itself. The copy is on our side, and only a developer can look.
- Tested 2 September 2026: the copy appeared 3 seconds after Save, with name, email, phone, address, city, postal code, notes and status all matching. Editing the phone in Zoho afterwards reached the copy in 2 seconds.
- If you want to be sure, ask for the row, or wait for the next sync alert email: if the sync had stalled, one would have arrived.

## If it did not work

- **Someone says the prospect is missing on our side.** The sync is behind or stopped. See [Fix a record that did not sync](/handbook/running/fix-a-record-that-did-not-sync).
- **The prospect shows as inactive.** You did not tick **Is Active**. Tick it in Zoho; the change crosses over.

## Good to know

- The **Source** you pick in Zoho does not cross over yet. Our side labels every Zoho-made prospect as if it came from the booking site. Harmless, but do not trust that column.
- A prospect created from the sales calendar widget arrives the same way, minus its email, which the widget cannot write yet.
