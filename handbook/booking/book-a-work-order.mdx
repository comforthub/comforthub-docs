You book from the customer's account in Zoho with the dispatch calendar widget. It asks for the job type, the equipment, the lines, the day and window, and the technician, then writes the work order and the appointment for you. The work order number comes from the engine, so it is never reused.

## Before you start

- The customer's account open in Zoho, with the property on it.
- The products you will put on the job already in the catalog; see [Add a product](/handbook/catalog-and-staff/add-a-product).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dispatch calendar">
    From the account, open the **Service Calendar** widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk the wizard">
    Pick the job type, tick the equipment (or add a new unit), pick the lines from the catalog and set who pays for each, choose the day and the arrival window, pick the technician, and choose whether the customer is emailed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The widget writes the work order, the equipment links and the appointment, and shows each step's result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Zoho has a work order named like WO458 with its lines, and an appointment under it.
- Tested 3 September 2026 with the same records the widget writes: the work order and its line were on our side in 2 seconds, with the line matched to the catalog item, and the appointment in 2 seconds with the technician, the window, the territory and the property.
- If you asked for an email, the customer has "Your Comfort Hub appointment on …" within a few seconds.

## Changing lines afterwards

Edit the lines on the work order in Zoho. Quantity and price changes reach our side in 3 seconds and the billing decision runs again. A line added on our side by the technician reaches Zoho in 2 seconds.
