Both are done on the appointment in Zoho. A move sends the customer a new notice; a new technician gets the stop on their phone.

## Before you start

- The appointment open in Zoho.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move it">
    Change **Scheduled Date** and, if needed, **Arrival Time Slot**. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restaff it">
    Change the technician in **Assigned Technicians**, and change one other field on the record with it (the widget does this for you). A technician change alone does not reach our side today; see below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Tested 3 September 2026: the new date was on our side in 2 seconds and "Your Comfort Hub appointment has moved to …" went out 1 second later. No second confirmation was sent.
- The new technician's phone gets a push, and the old one no longer sees the stop. Needs a technician with the app.

## If it did not work

- **The technician did not change on our side.** Only the technician was changed. Change any other field on the appointment and save again. Known, being fixed.
