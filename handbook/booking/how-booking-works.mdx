A job starts as a **work order** in Zoho with its lines, its equipment and one or more **appointments**. The dispatch calendar widget writes all of that in one go; the engine copies each piece to our side within seconds and does three things with it.

## What the engine does with a booking

| Piece | Reaches our side | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Work order and lines | 2 s | the billing decision runs |
| Equipment on the job | when the work order is saved | linked to the job here |
| Appointment | 2 s | technician, window, territory and property set; the booking email goes out |
| Checklist on the appointment | when the appointment is saved | becomes an assignment matched to the right equipment |

Tested 3 September 2026 on WO458 and WO459.

## The billing decision

Every line is billed to the customer or to warranty (and, for plan customers, to the subscription). The engine looks at the lines the moment the work order arrives and again on every line change, and writes the answer back to Zoho's **Billing Status** within 2 seconds:

- Any line billed to the customer: **Draft**, to be invoiced on completion.
- Only warranty lines: **Non-Billable**. No invoice will ever be made.
- Only plan lines: **Non-Billable** too. A line counts as a plan line when the work order names the customer's subscription (or the customer has exactly one live plan), the plan includes that kind of visit, and the line is the matching maintenance product: Routine Furnace Maintenance for a heating visit, Routine Condenser Maintenance for a cooling visit, Routine Tankless Maintenance for a tankless visit. Any other line on a plan customer's job bills as usual.
- A plan job is also written onto the customer's subscription in Zoho as a visit (**Subscription Visits**: the service, Scheduled, and the work order). When the job completes, the visit turns Completed with the date.

Tested 4 September 2026: a warranty-only job and a plan-only job (one Routine Furnace Maintenance line on an All-Season customer) were both **Non-Billable** on our side the second they arrived, and Zoho showed it 5 seconds later. The plan job's visit was on the subscription in Zoho one second after the job arrived. That visit appeared twice (the same service, same job) because the job and its line arrived in the same second and were logged by two hands at once; the next logging pass on that job folds them into one. Known, reported for filing.

## Emails and pushes

- With **Notify Client** on, the customer gets "Your Comfort Hub appointment on …" within 2 seconds, with an add-to-calendar link.
- Moving the appointment sends "has moved to …" and sends no second confirmation.
- The technician's phone gets a push for a new, moved or cancelled appointment. That needs the technician to have the app; see [Add a technician](/handbook/catalog-and-staff/add-a-technician).

## Two things to know

- **Line descriptions.** What you type on a line is replaced by the catalog item's description on both sides. Known, being fixed.
- **A work order typed straight into Zoho makes no equipment** for a furnace or condenser on its lines. Only the dispatch widget does that today (COM-181). Book installs from the widget.
- **Changing only the technician, equipment or checklist** on a saved appointment does not reach our side until some other field on the record changes too. The widget saves the date and window with every change, so it is fine there. Known, being fixed.
