Cancel the work order in Zoho and the engine cleans up behind it: appointments are cancelled here and in Zoho, checklists that were never started are removed, and an invoice that was already made is voided.

## Before you start

- The work order open in Zoho.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the status">
    Set **Status** to **Cancelled** and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Tested 3 September 2026 on WO459: the work order was cancelled on our side in 2 seconds, its appointment was cancelled here and written back to Zoho as Cancelled, and its pending checklist was gone.
- The technician's phone gets a cancelled push.
- If the job was already invoiced, the invoice is voided; see [Void an invoice](/handbook/invoicing/void-an-invoice).
