The invoice email carries a pay link. The customer opens it, sees the invoice, and pays by card or bank transfer inside Helcim's payment window. The engine marks the invoice Paid the moment Helcim approves the card, and the rest follows on its own; see [How payments work](/handbook/payments/how-payments-work).

## Before you start

- An invoice that has been sent, with a balance owing. The customer needs the pay link from the invoice email.
- Nothing to open on our side. The page does the work.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pay link">
    The page shows the invoice number, the amount due, the due date, the items and the balance. A paid, void or draft invoice shows its badge and no way to pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Pay invoice">
    If the invoice allows part payment, an amount box appears first with the balance remaining under it. Helcim's payment window opens over the invoice; the customer pays by card or by bank transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    A **Paid** badge means Helcim approved the card and the engine recorded it; **Partially paid** means something is still owed. **Declined** means the card was refused and the invoice stays Due. A bank transfer shows **Processing** until it clears, days later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- The page shows **Paid**, and our copy of the invoice reads Paid with nothing due.
- Within a few hours the customer has "Payment received for INV…" with the receipt attached, the work order reads Paid in the app and in Zoho, Zoho has one Payment on the invoice, and QuickBooks shows exactly one payment on it, posted by Helcim.
- The pay link is never guessable: it is a random slug minted by the engine, not a Helcim id.

## If it did not work

- **Declined**: nothing was recorded. The customer can try again from the same link.
- The invoice reads Paid here but no receipt has arrived after two hours: Helcim did not post the payment into QuickBooks, so the settle job gave up and the monitor opened an urgent condition in Sentry. See [Act on a Sentry email](/handbook/running/act-on-a-sentry-email).
- A bank transfer is settled only when it clears. Nothing is wrong while it shows **Processing**.
