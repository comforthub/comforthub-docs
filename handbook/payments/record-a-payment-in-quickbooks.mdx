A cheque, a transfer or a card taken over the phone is recorded in QuickBooks against the invoice. Everything else follows from there.

## Before you start

- QuickBooks open. The invoice number.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive the payment">
    In QuickBooks, receive a payment for the customer, tick the invoice, enter the amount, and save. A part payment is fine.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Tested 3 September 2026: within 13 seconds our side had the payment and its receipt, the invoice state was right (Partial after $2, Paid after the rest), Zoho had the payment, and on the closing payment the customer got "Payment received for INV…".
