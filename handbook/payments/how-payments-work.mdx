QuickBooks is the one that tells us a payment happened. Whether the customer paid on the pay page (Helcim) or the office recorded a payment in QuickBooks, the same thing follows: QuickBooks sends us the payment, and the engine does the rest.

## What follows a payment

Seen on 3 September 2026, two payments on a $5.65 invoice:

| After | Our side | Zoho | Email |
|---|---|---|---|
| $2.00, first | payment recorded with its receipt PDF; invoice Partial, $3.65 due | a Payment on the invoice | none, on purpose |
| $3.65, second | invoice Paid, paid-on date set; work order Paid | a second Payment; the paid PDF attached to the invoice | "Payment received for INV…" with the receipt |

Each step landed within 13 seconds of the payment.

## The Zoho half, watched once more

Seen 4 September 2026 on a fresh probe customer, invoice INV008348 for $5.65, paid $2.00 then $3.65 in QuickBooks:

| After | In Zoho | How long |
|---|---|---|
| $2.00 | a Payment named "INV008348 Payment", $2, Cash, linked to the invoice and the account; the invoice PDF and a paid copy attached to the invoice | 8 seconds |
| $3.65 | a second Payment, $3.65; the invoice's **Payment Status** Paid; the work order's **Billing Status** Paid | 9 seconds for the Payment, 15 for the work order |

On our side the invoice went Partial with $3.65 owing after the first payment and Paid after the second, each with its receipt PDF stored, and the receipt email left 21 seconds after the second payment. No receipt after the first, on purpose. The payment method in Zoho is the one chosen in QuickBooks (Cash, Cheque, Credit Card or e-Transfer); a payment recorded with any other method is labelled Credit Card and flagged for a person.

## Edits and deletes in Zoho

Our side owns the amount and the method on a payment. A method changed here was in Zoho within 3 seconds (seen 4 September 2026); a method typed in Zoho is not applied here and is overwritten at the next push, and nothing flags it. Deleting a Payment in Zoho changes nothing here or in QuickBooks: a human-review flag is raised within seconds and the payment stands. See [Resolve a human-review flag](/handbook/running/resolve-a-human-review-flag).

## The pay page

The pay link in the invoice email opens the invoice with its lines, total and PDF. Paying there records the payment in QuickBooks, and QuickBooks tells us as above.
