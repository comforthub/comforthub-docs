Every person in the app has one of five roles, on a ladder. The engine checks the ladder on every write. The app does not yet grey out or hide anything by role, so a write above your rank looks like it saved and does nothing.

## The ladder

| Role | Rung | What the engine lets them do |
|---|---|---|
| apprentice | 1 | See what staff see. Change nothing measured below. |
| technician | 2 | Change customers, quotes, purchase orders, work orders and appointments, on any work order, not only their own. |
| senior technician | 3 | Everything a technician can, and more. Not measured on 4 September 2026. |
| admin | 4 | Not measured on 4 September 2026. |
| super admin | 5 | Not measured on 4 September 2026. |

Only the owner can change someone's role; see [Invite someone to the app](/handbook/app/invite-someone-to-the-app).

## What happens when a write is above your rank

Measured 4 September 2026 (<Linear issue="COM-57" />), each write run against the engine directly and rolled back, so no record changed.

| Write | Signed in as a technician | Signed in below technician rank |
|---|---|---|
| Edit a customer | Saved, 1 row | Nothing saved, no error, 0 rows |
| Add a quote | Saved | Refused with an error from the engine |
| Raise a purchase order | Saved | Refused with an error from the engine |
| Edit a work order you are not on | Saved, 1 row | Nothing saved, no error, 0 rows |
| Mark a no-show | Saved, 1 row | Nothing saved, no error, 0 rows |

Two things to know about that table:

- Nobody held the apprentice role on 4 September (one super admin, two technicians), so the right-hand column was measured with a signed-in account that has no staff row, which sits on rung 0. The apprentice rung, 1, is below the technician rung, 2, on the same ladder, so the engine gives the same answers.
- Edits above your rank match no rows and come back as success; that is why the app shows nothing. Adding a record above your rank is refused with an error, which the app may or may not show. Disabling the control with a reason is app work not yet done; that is the gap in <Linear issue="COM-57" />.
