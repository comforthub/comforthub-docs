Three switches decide whether a caller reaches a queue or hears the closed greeting: the closed switch for the whole line, the holiday list, and each department's weekly hours. All three live in the engine's phone settings. The phone reads them fresh every 30 seconds, so a change is live within half a minute.

## Before you start

- You can edit the engine's phone settings. The app shows the holiday list but cannot change any of the three.
- You know which department the change is for. Sales and other matters keep weekday hours, 8 am to 5 pm Toronto time. Support is marked always open and ignores hours and holidays.
- Times are Toronto time, whatever the caller's own clock says.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Close the whole line right away">
    Turn on the closed switch. Every caller to the main line hears the closed greeting and is asked to leave a message. A technician's personal line still rings its owner. Turn the switch off again when you are back; nothing else resets it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a holiday">
    Add the date, for every department or for one. On that day the menu still plays, and a caller who picks a department on holiday hears the closed greeting and voicemail. A department with no holiday that day answers as usual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a department's weekly hours">
    Each department has an opening and a closing time per weekday. Outside those times the caller who picks that department hears the closed greeting and voicemail. A weekday with no hours is closed all day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- Wait 30 seconds, then call the line and pick the department. With the closed switch on, the closed greeting plays before the menu. With a holiday or closed hours, the menu plays and the closed greeting follows your choice.
- The call is in the CRM's call history within a minute.

## If it did not work

- Wait 30 seconds and call again; the phone caches its settings for that long.
- Check the closed switch is off. It stays on until someone turns it off.
- A holiday for one department does not close the others.
