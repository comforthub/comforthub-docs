Three switches decide whether a caller reaches a queue or hears the closed greeting: the closed switch for the whole line, the holiday list, and each department's weekly hours. The phone reads them fresh every 30 seconds, so a change is live within half a minute.

## Before you start

- You are a super admin.
- You know which department the change is for. Sales and other matters keep office hours (Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm Toronto time); support is marked always open and ignores hours and holidays.
- Times are Toronto time, whatever the caller's own clock says.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Close the whole line right now">
    Turn on the closed switch in the phone settings. Every caller to the main line hears "Our office is currently closed" and is asked to leave a message. A technician's personal line still rings its owner. Turn the switch off again when you are back; nothing else resets it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a holiday">
    Add the date with a label, for every department or for one. On that day the menu still plays, and a caller who picks a department on holiday hears the closed greeting and voicemail. A department with no holiday that day answers as usual. The next 20 statutory days to the end of 2027 are already loaded. A holiday can be read from the app but is added in the engine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a department's weekly hours">
    Each department has an opening and a closing time per weekday in the phone settings. Outside those times the caller who picks that department hears the closed greeting and voicemail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

Tested 4 September 2026 (<Linear issue="COM-98" />) by replaying the calls Twilio would send, from a made-up caller named "Probe simulator":

- Closed switch on: a call to the main line was answered with the closed greeting and the voicemail prompt in under 1 second. The voicemail was on our side 1 second after the caller finished, and the call was in Zoho's call history 1 second after it began.
- Holiday on "other matters" for that day: pressing 3 gave the closed greeting and voicemail. In the same minute, pressing 1 for sales, which keeps the same hours but had no holiday, went to the sales queue.
- Each change was picked up on the next call after 30 seconds.
- The weekly hours were not changed during that test, so the hours check itself was not re-timed that day.

## If it did not work

- Wait 30 seconds and call again; the phone caches its settings for that long.
- Check the closed switch is off. It stays on until someone turns it off.
- A holiday for one department does not close the others.
