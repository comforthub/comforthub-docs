A caller can ring one agent two ways: press star on the main menu and dial an extension, or call the agent's own number. Either way the agent's app rings for 25 seconds, and an unanswered call becomes a voicemail.

## Before you start

- The agent has an extension on their app user record. An admin sets it; nobody can set their own. Two people with the same extension ring nobody.
- The agent is an active phone agent and signed in to the app.
- For a personal line: a second number on the Twilio account, with that agent as its owner in the engine.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dial an extension from the menu">
    The caller presses star during the greeting, hears **Please enter the extension, followed by the pound key**, and types the extension (up to six digits) and pound. The agent's app rings. An unknown extension hears **Sorry, that extension is not in service** and lands back on the menu; that counts as one of the menu's three tries, and after the third the line says goodbye and hangs up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call a personal line">
    A number that has an owner rings only that owner, even when the closed switch is on. The menu does not play and the queues are not offered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Nobody answers">
    After 25 seconds the caller hears the voicemail prompt. A message left on a personal line is filed under that line, not the main one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you know it worked

- The agent's app rings within a few seconds of the extension or the number being dialled, and only that agent's app.
- The call is in the CRM's call history within a minute, naming the agent who answered.
- A missed call on a personal line shows up as a voicemail under that line, with a **Missed call** push only when no message was left.

## If it did not work

- The caller hears **not in service**: the extension is missing, is on two people, or the person is not an active phone agent. Fix the extension on the app user record or the phone agent record.
- The app did not ring: the agent is signed out. Sign in and try again.

## Good to know

- A call to a personal line is not recorded, and the caller is not told it might be.
